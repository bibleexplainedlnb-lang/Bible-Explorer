You are building SEO-optimized internal linking for a Bible content website.

## GOAL:

Maximize internal linking WITHOUT restriction, while keeping relevance, readability, and structure clean.

---

## CORE RULES:

### 1. PILLAR → CHILD (HIGHEST PRIORITY)

* If article is about a main topic (e.g., Love)
* Then include ALL relevant child pages inside content:

  * Love in Marriage
  * Unconditional Love
  * Love and Forgiveness
  * Love and Patience
  * Love and Kindness
  * etc.

✔ Add naturally:

* Intro section
* Mid content (after 1st or 2nd paragraph)
* End section

---

### 2. MID-CONTENT LINKING (MANDATORY)

* Insert links BETWEEN paragraphs (not inside long sentences)
* Max 1–2 links per paragraph block
* Anchor text = natural phrase (NOT forced keyword stuffing)

---

### 3. WITHIN CONTENT (CONTROLLED)

* Allow linking inside sentences BUT:
  ❌ No long anchor text
  ❌ No full sentence linking
  ✔ Max 3–5 words anchor

Example:
✔ "Bible verses about love and forgiveness"
❌ "If you are struggling with emotional pain and need guidance then read this article about..."

---

### 4. BOTTOM SECTION (STRUCTURED)

Add:

#### 🔹 Related Verses

* Same topic (child pages first)
* Then pillar
* Then category

#### 🔹 Explore More

* 1–2 links from closely related category

Example:

* Love → link to Faith / Forgiveness
* Children → link to Parenting / Wisdom

---

### 5. CATEGORY LEVEL LINKING

* Add 2–3 links from same category
* Add 1–2 links from close relevant category

---

### 6. NO LINKING RESTRICTION

* Add as many links as naturally possible
* Prioritize:

  1. Child pages
  2. Same topic
  3. Same category
  4. Related category

---

### 7. ANCHOR TEXT CONTROL (VERY IMPORTANT)

❌ Avoid:

* Long sentences as links
* Random words
* Forced keywords

✔ Use:

* Clean phrases (2–5 words)
* Natural reading flow

---

### 8. ADMIN INTERLINKING LIMIT REMOVE

* Do NOT limit to 16 links
* Add all relevant links
* System should not restrict count

---

## OUTPUT FORMAT:

* Clean HTML
* Proper paragraph spacing
* Links distributed across:
  ✔ intro
  ✔ mid
  ✔ bottom

---

## FINAL GOAL:

* Maximum internal linking
* No spam
* No awkward sentences
* Strong topical authority

---

## BONUS RULE:

If pillar exists → ALWAYS push child links strongly inside content.
